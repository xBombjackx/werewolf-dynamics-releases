The modules

1. Console, Channel & Shared Chassis

WD Rack — The hub of the shared-memory chassis. Load WD Rack on any track or master bus to inspect every linked Werewolf Dynamics plugin in your session, check backplane power draw and slot bleed, and flip global ALL ON / BYPASS or DELTA ALL across all linked instances simultaneously. Runs standalone with zero configuration if no other modules are loaded.

WD Strip — Complete analog channel strip combining the 7-law WD Pre input stage, a musical 4-band console equalizer, and the WD Comp dynamics bay (switchable optical, FET, variable-mu, and VCA cards) into one transformer-coupled output stage.

WD Pre — Standalone console preamp featuring 7 selectable circuit architectures (Precision, Class A 73, Op-Amp 12, Tube 76, Porta 4-Track, Clean E, Tube Tone), extended upper-knob stem overdrive, resonant HP/LP filters, phase flip, and automatic loudness MATCH.

WD Summer & WD Summer Channel — Drop WD Summer Channel onto individual tracks or stems and insert WD Summer on your mix bus to pass audio through 5 selectable analog summing desks (WD-N Brit Iron, WD-S Big Clean, WD-A US Steel, WD-T Tube Console, WD-G German Broadcast) with shared bus supply sag, inter-channel crosstalk, warm stem overdrive, and loudness-neutralizing auto-gain.

WD 361 — Multi-character compander repurposed as a bright, breathy vocal and acoustic air enhancer. Switch across three companding TYPE architectures (Type A 4-band encode/decode, wide-band linear companding, and Type B single-band HF emphasis) plus full/low/high band pairing to lift shimmer and presence dynamically.

2. Compressors & Dynamics

WD Opto — Feedback optical leveling amplifier built around a physical CdS/EL photocell model with fast/slow carrier memory, deep-trap program release, T4A and T4B cell flavors, deterministic Unit 1–8 calibration spread, and tube or class-AB driver stages.

WD FET — Snap-fast peak limiter with microsecond attack transients, classic ratio steps plus all-buttons-in overload mode, deterministic Unit 1–8 component tolerance, sidechain high-pass, and output transformer drive.

WD Mu — Dual-triode variable-mu bus and mastering compressor delivering smooth, wide-shoulder leveling, push-pull transformer magnetics, linked or dual-mono channels, and balanced odd/even tube coloration.

WD VCA — Tight, punchy RMS-detected mix-bus compressor with stepped ratios, program-dependent auto release, internal sidechain high-pass filtering, and selectable discrete/op-amp bus stages for cohesive drum and full-mix glue.

WD Clamp & WD Duress — Two sides of modern studio dynamics. WD Clamp is a 3-slot serial dynamics rack sharing one sagged power rail and inter-slot crosstalk — assign Opto, Mu, FET, or VCA cards across slots A, B, and C in any of 6 chain orders, with a selectable 0 / -10 / -20 dB input pad and GR Follow or Match auto-gain. WD Duress delivers aggressive parallel drum smash with 5 ratio characters up to brick-wall Nuke, zero-mean 2nd- and 3rd-order harmonic generators, detector high-pass emphasis, and dry/wet punch.

3. Equalizers & Harmonic Shaping

WD Tec — Dual-section passive inductor-capacitor program equalizer combining simultaneous low boost/attenuate, high boost/attenuate, and mid-frequency dip/peak stages driven through a full vacuum-tube makeup amplifier.

WD XQ — Multi-architecture stereo mastering equalizer switching across 5 console and passive circuit voices (Precision, Console E, Console G, Discrete 550, Inductor 73) with proportional/constant-Q bands, per-band dynamic compression/expansion, stereo/dual-mono/M-S routing, and a built-in MATCH curve matcher to learn and transfer spectral balance from any reference source.

WD AX — Harmonic exciter and low-end phase aligner adding crisp upper-register presence and tight low-end weight without clouding the midrange.

4. Tape, Delay, Reverb & Stereo

WD Tape — Physical magnetic tape recorder modeling four transport speeds (30, 15, 7.5, and 3.75 ips), multiple tape formulations, Jiles-Atherton hysteresis saturation, low-end head bump, bias calibration, flutter/wow, and calibrated tape hiss.

WD Echo — Multi-head analog tape echo unit with selectable playhead combinations, BPM tempo sync, gritty self-oscillating feedback, transport wow/flutter, and an integrated spring-flavored reverb tank.

WD Brigade — Analog bucket-brigade delay, chorus, and flanger modeling BBD clock aliasing, compander pumping, dark feedback repeats, BPM tempo sync, and wide stereo modulation.

WD Spring — Physical spring reverb offering three tank geometries (Combo, Outboard, and Tall) with authentic dispersion chirp, metallic spring bounce, tank input saturation, stereo tension modulation, and high/low decay damping.

WD Span — Difference-signal magnetic stereo widener that pushes your Side signal through a physical transformer core (Iron) with unity-exact bypass, sub-bass fold-down (Mono Maker), spectral Shuffle tilt, and stereo width control.

5. Chain Auditioning

WD Delta Send & WD Delta Return — Insert WD Delta Send before any chain of third-party plugins and WD Delta Return afterward to automatically measure chain latency via cross-correlation, match loudness, and solo the pure delta difference or blend wet/dry across an entire FX rack. Engage LOOK (850 ms lookahead) so level-matched A/B swaps (WET MATCH / DELTA MATCH) have zero loudness excursion when an upstream plugin changes.

6. Included in Preview Mode (New in v0.9.0)

Preview Mode plugins are fully functional and bundled in all v0.9.0 downloads for everyday mixing while their visuals and edge behaviors continue to be refined toward v1.0.

WD Horse Girl — Intelligent 4-zone spectral mask and priority sculptor. Pair instances across competing tracks across LO / HI, MID / SIDE, or LEFT / RIGHT axes in GLUE or SEPARATE mode — live energy blobs and partner listener meters show exactly where tracks carve room for one another.

WD Damper — Adaptive resonance suppressor that distinguishes sharp, narrow ringing lines from wide, intentional musical humps — smoothing harsh peaks without stripping weight or vocal body.

WD Annihilator — 3-band multiband distortion laboratory letting Low, Mid, and High bands each run separate analog saturation circuits (triode current starve/overfeed, diode voltage sag, negative iron, tape, or wreck) in Stereo or Mid/Side routing under one shared power supply.

WD VU — Calibrated analog ballistic VU and PPM meter bridge with selectable studio headroom reference points and peak telemetry.

Download & install

  • Windows (64-bit VST3 + CLAP): Run WerewolfDynamics-v0.9.0-Windows-Installer.exe for automated installation, or download WerewolfDynamics-v0.9.0-Windows-VST3-CLAP.zip below and copy the .vst3 and .clap bundles into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP.
  • macOS (Universal VST3 + AU): Download WerewolfDynamics-v0.9.0-macOS-VST3-AU.zip (mac channel below or GitHub Releases v0.9.0) and copy into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (or the user library equivalents). These builds are not notarized by Apple, so run the single xattr command printed inside INSTALL.txt in the archive after copying.
  • Linux (x86_64 VST3 + CLAP Beta): Download the Linux beta tarball on the linux-beta channel below or from GitHub Releases and extract into ~/.vst3 and ~/.clap. Requires WebKitGTK 4.1 (libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0 on Debian/Ubuntu) for plugin UI rendering.

Host notes & accuracy

  • Session compatibility: All 22 core plugins retain identical plugin IDs, frozen parameter IDs, and frozen parameter order so saved DAW sessions reload cleanly. Perform a one-time plugin rescan after upgrading so your DAW discovers the 4 new Preview Mode plugins.
  • Latency & PDC behavior: 25 of the 26 plugins hold constant latency across all parameter moves during playback so host plugin delay compensation stays steady without mid-playback dropouts. Only WD Delta Return updates its reported delay (on the message thread) when you toggle LOOK (0 vs. 850 ms lookahead).
  • Code signing: Windows and macOS releases are currently unsigned/un-notarized — SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may ask you to confirm on first launch.
  • Tip — direct keyboard entry: Double-click any parameter value readout to type an exact number (supports k, kHz, Hz, dB, ms, s, %).
  • Technical documentation & feedback: Circuit assumptions, benchmark methodology, and calibration details are documented in ACCURACY.md. Found a bug or DAW quirk? Open a report on the Werewolf Dynamics issue tracker.
