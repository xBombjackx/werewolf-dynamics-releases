Analog gear, simulated from the circuit up

Twenty-two free audio plugins that solve the actual circuit
while your audio runs through it.

Download — free ▶ Hear it in your browser Latest release

Most "analog modeled" plugins are a snapshot: measure the hardware, fit some curves, ship the curves. These solve the actual circuit while your audio runs through it. The tube bias shifts when you hit it hard. The transformer core saturates from flux, so it cares about frequency, not just level. The opto cell remembers what you played ten seconds ago. Nothing is drawn on; if a meter moves or a curve bends, something in the circuit did it.

And since the whole circuit is in there, you get the screwdriver too. Every module has a rear panel with the service trims the hardware kept locked inside: supply draw, bias, transformer headroom, tube swaps. Set them to factory and it behaves like a well-kept unit. Or don't.

Hear it first

Fourteen A/B demos, one mechanism each — sample-locked performances where switching circuits mid-playback is a 12 ms crossfade, so what changes is only the circuit. Loudness-matched, no mastering, no cheating. Plus two plugins running live in your browser.

The lineup

WD OPTO

Compressor · optical tube

An LA-2A-style optical compressor. The photocell has memory, so the release depends on what you played.

WD MU

Compressor · variable-mu

A tube compressor built around a simulated 6386 push-pull stage. Drive it and it leans back.

WDTEC

Equalizer · passive tube

A Pultec-style program EQ plus midrange section. The boost you cannot get wrong.

WD TAPE

Tape machine

A full tape machine: record head, bias oscillator, reproduce head. Aligned the way a tech would.

WD XQ

Equalizer · parametric, five circuits

One parametric EQ, five circuits under it. The knobs stay put while the circuit changes what they do.

WD FET

Compressor · FET

A 1176-style FET compressor with four circuit laws. Attack is measured in microseconds.

WD VCA

Compressor · VCA bus

A stereo bus compressor on one Blackmer-style gain cell, with three detector brains.

WD DURESS

Compressor · curve family

A Distressor-style compressor. Every ratio position is its own engineered curve.

WD CLAMP

Compressor rack · three slots

Three compressor slots on one power supply. Drive one hard and its neighbors feel it.

WD PRE

Preamp · five circuits

Five preamps behind one gain knob. Every detent is a slightly different amplifier.

WD ECHO

Delay · tape loop echo

A tape loop echo where the loop is real enough to have a splice.

WD AX

Exciter

An Aphex-style exciter built from the patents. It adds a sideband instead of boosting EQ.

WD RACK

Chassis · cross-plugin coupling

The chassis. Give your modules the same frame number and they share a power supply.

WD STRIP

Channel strip

A console channel in one window: preamp, five desk EQs, the desk channel comp, on one power rail.

WD SUMMER CHANNEL

Console channel

The desk channel, one per track. Five consoles on one selector.

WD SUMMER

Console bus · summing

The bus those channels sum into. A busy mix sags the shared supply.

WD SPAN

Stereo widener · transformer

A widener that works with a transformer instead of phase. The mono fold stays exact.

WD SPRING

Reverb · spring tank

A real spring tank. The boing is a mechanism, not a filter.

WD 361

Compander · encode processor

The tape-side half of a noise reduction unit, used the way engineers misused it.

WD BRIGADE

Delay · bucket brigade

A BBD delay, dark for the same reason the originals were. The delay time IS the clock.

WD DELTA SEND / RETURN

Monitoring utility · chain difference

Hear what your whole chain is doing. Any plugins, anyone\u2019s.

How do we know it's accurate?

ACCURACY.md explains the whole method: the published numbers we calibrate against, the automated bench that measures every build, and the audit that checked every plugin against its hardware's spec sheets. Where a number isn't published, the docs say so and say what we chose.

Requirements

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) with any VST3 or CLAP host — an installer is included. macOS 10.15 (Catalina) and newer with any VST3 or AU host; one universal binary covers Apple Silicon and Intel. Free for any use, commercial included; please link people here instead of re-hosting the files.
