Most "analog modeled" plugins are a snapshot: measure hardware once, fit some static waveshapers, and draw a faceplate over it. Werewolf Dynamics plugins solve the actual circuit while your audio runs through it. Tube bias shifts when you lean on a rail. Transformer cores saturate from magnetic flux, so low end bends first. Photocells remember what you played ten seconds ago. Nothing is drawn on — if a meter moves or a transfer curve bends, something in the circuit caused it.

And because the whole circuit is in there, you get the service trimpots too. Flip to the rear panel on core modules to adjust supply sag, bias trim, calibration drive, tube matching, or chassis coupling. Keep them at factory calibration or pull units out of spec.

Requirements & Host Notes

Windows 10/11 (64-bit): VST3 + CLAP + automated installer. macOS 10.15+: Universal VST3 + AU binary (not yet notarized; clear quarantine with the single command in INSTALL.txt). Linux (x86_64 Beta): VST3 + CLAP, glibc 2.35+, requires WebKitGTK 4.1 (libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0). Latency: 29 of 30 plugins hold constant latency across all parameter moves during playback; only WD Delta Return updates reported delay when toggling LOOK (850 ms).
